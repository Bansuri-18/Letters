The day we met and all this happened

Hey you,

Honestly, when I swiped right, I didn’t really think much of it. It was more of a gut feeling… and maybe the fact that I found you a little cute had something to do with it. But then I actually met you, and you were so much better than I’d expected, to be honest.

Although, I have a feeling the formals might have played a tiny role in that. I’m an absolute sucker for guys in formals, and I’ll admit, I do judge a little based on what someone wears. So, I guess you scored some extra brownie points there. And I definitely wasn’t expecting you to be that tall either! But hey, I guess this means I’ll be wearing my favourite heels around you more often, and you’ll have the very important responsibility of making sure I don’t trip, fall, or embarrass myself. Basically, you’re signing up to be my personal safety net.

And can we talk about your voice for a second? You have such a lovely, captivating voice. I genuinely feel like I could sit and listen to you talk for hours, even if you were talking about the most random things. There’s just something about it.

Also, I really appreciated you showing up that day, especially after such a long day of your own. I don’t think I properly expressed it, but it genuinely meant a lot to have you around. I usually go to that place when I’m feeling a little low, and somehow, without even knowing it, you managed to lift my mood that day. You made an already difficult day feel a little lighter, and I think that’s something worth telling you.

You also have this really bubbly, endearing personality that I quite like. I’m genuinely impressed by how straightforward you are about things, and I love how you seem to balance being mature and serious when you need to be with this playful, almost childlike side of you. It’s honestly quite refreshing.

And I guess this is where I get a little vulnerable, so bear with me for a second.

I’ve been told quite a few times that I’m not the easiest person to get to know because I take my time opening up to people. Trusting someone and genuinely connecting with them doesn’t come very easily to me. And honestly, that’s pretty much how things have been for me here so far.

I’ve gone out with a few people before you (four, to be precise), but I always felt like I was holding a little part of myself back. I’d think before I spoke, carefully choose what to say, ask all the polite little questions, give the appropriate answers, and wear that picture-perfect version of myself. You know, the fake smiles, the perfectly courteous conversations, the whole I have my life together and am a perfectly composed human being act.

But with you, it felt different. I wasn’t constantly thinking about how I came across or trying to make sure I said all the right things. I could just be myself. No carefully curated personality, no pretending, no pressure to be the perfect version of me. Just me, being me.

And I think that’s what I like most about being around you so far. You make it feel okay to let my inner child out, to be a little silly, a little goofy, and just exist without overthinking every little thing. I don’t feel like I have to hide that side of myself or worry that it’s too much. And honestly, finding someone who makes me feel that comfortable, even this early on, is quite special.

I genuinely didn’t want to leave that night. I didn’t even realise how quickly the time had passed until it was time to go. It’s funny how some conversations can make hours feel like minutes, isn’t it?

And, well… you’ve been on my mind ever since. I find myself wanting to know more about you, discover all the little things that make you who you are, and see where this goes. I’m curious about us, about how we might fit into each other’s lives, and about what could come out of this if we give it a chance.

Gosh, why does this sound like I’m halfway through confessing my feelings to you? I swear I wasn’t planning to get this mushy, but apparently, you bring that side out of me too.

Anyway, I’ll stop before I embarrass myself any further. Let’s just take things as they come, get to know each other a little more, and see where this little thing between us takes us.

But for now, I’ll just say this:
I’m really glad I swiped right.

The day we went out to the beach

Hey you,

Today honestly felt like something out of a dream. The beach, our little walks, holding hands, the fireworks, and even getting on the wrong bus. Everything felt so surreal that I still can’t quite believe it happened. And okay, I admit, I was a little excited to see your reaction to my outfit, which is why I didn't mention anything. I guess it was worth it.

I’ve always loved that beach, but today, for the first time, my attention was on something else entirely. You. And when you held my hand, it just felt right. I can’t really explain it, but even though I’m a little scared of the water, walking beside you with my hand in yours made everything feel okay. I was so giddy and shy the entire time, barely able to look into your eyes because I was too busy blushing and getting flustered. You genuinely made me feel like a teenager crushing on her favourite fictional character all over again.

You’re incredibly handsome, and I could probably go on about your eyes and your smile for hours, but what really gets me is the person you are. You’re so kind and caring, and there’s something about the way you speak that draws me in. Sometimes, I find it genuinely difficult to look away from you.

And when you told me that whenever you helped someone and looked back at me, I was always smiling at you, it made me smile all over again. Because how could I not? There I was, watching you be your lovely, thoughtful self, feeling ridiculously lucky that I get to call you mine.

I think that’s what makes today so special to me. It wasn’t just the pretty beach or the fireworks; it was getting to experience all those little moments with you. The kind of moments I know I’ll look back on and smile about for a very long time.

Gosh, I’m getting so mushy. I’ve never been very good at talking about my feelings, so I end up writing them instead, hoping that someday I’ll find the courage to say all of this while looking you in the eyes. But how am I supposed to look directly at the sun when it’s the very thing making my world feel warmer?

It reminds me of this quote I once read about someone who spent so long looking away from the sun that they only realised its beauty when it was setting. And I think there’s something beautiful and heartbreaking about that. I never want to take what we have for granted or wait until a moment is passing to realise how precious it is. I want to appreciate you, all the little things about you, and the moments we get to share while we have them.

So, I hope you don’t mind if I sometimes struggle to put my feelings into words or take a little longer to open up. It’s always been difficult for me, but what I feel is genuine, even when I don’t know how to express it.

And if I’m being honest, out of all the possibilities of where this could go,
I really, really hope that the person I end up with is you.

Quantum entanglement & a red string

Hey you,

I’ve been meaning to write this, but I guess letting everything out yesterday helped me release most of the emotions I’d been holding in. I think I was just overwhelmed with everything happening in my life, and since I tend to bottle things up rather than let them out, it all got a little too much yesterday.

Plus, all the dust I inhaled on my way home probably triggered my allergies. I was feeling dizzy, cold, and had a runny nose — the usual signs that my allergies are acting up. And I swear, I’m probably at my most vulnerable and emotional when I’m sick… or drunk. The fact that I almost cried on the bus is still unbelievable to me. Thankfully, Mum didn’t notice my voice breaking, or I probably would’ve ended up crying on the phone with her too.

I feel much better now after taking my meds and getting some sleep, so don’t worry too much about me, okay? But I do feel bad for bringing your mood down yesterday, and I’m really sorry about that. I never meant to. Though, I guess there’s one tiny silver lining to all this — now you know how I feel whenever you tell me you’re feeling feverish or have body aches. Maybe this is my way of understanding you a little better, even if I wish it hadn’t happened this way.

Also, I was reading a paper yesterday and came across something that made me think of you: quantum entanglement. And somehow, my little science-loving heart turned it into something romantic.

In quantum physics, entanglement is when two particles become connected in such a way that they can no longer be described entirely independently of each other, even when they’re separated by enormous distances. Their properties are linked in a way that makes their connection truly fascinating.

And I don’t know why, but it reminded me of us. Of how two people can be living their own separate lives, carrying their own thoughts, worries, and little worlds, and then somehow meet and start becoming a part of each other’s lives. Suddenly, when one of them is having a difficult day, the other feels it too. When one is happy, the other finds themselves smiling. And even when they’re apart, they still find little ways of being present in each other’s minds.

It also reminded me of the red string of fate — the belief that two people destined to meet are connected by an invisible red thread, one that might stretch or tangle but will never truly break, no matter how far apart they are or how long it takes for them to find each other.

I know quantum entanglement is science, and the red string is a beautiful old belief, but I like imagining them together. One explains a mysterious connection between particles, while the other gives that connection a story about people and fate. And maybe that’s why I find them so beautiful.

Because sometimes, I wonder how two people who were once complete strangers can suddenly become someone the other thinks about so often. How someone who wasn't even part of your life a little while ago can start affecting your mood, making you smile at your phone, or making you wish you could be beside them when things get difficult.

And maybe we don't need to call it fate or try to explain it just yet. Maybe we're simply two people who happened to find each other, slowly learning how to care for one another. But I do like the thought that somewhere along the way, our little worlds have started becoming connected. That even as we continue living our separate lives, there's now this tiny thread between us, tying together little moments, feelings, and memories that are becoming ours.

And if the universe really does have its own way of bringing people together, then I think I'm quite happy that, out of all the people and all the possibilities, somehow, I got to meet you.

Maybe we're not quantum-entangled particles, and maybe there's no magical red thread around our pinkies. But if there's one thing I know, it's that you've started becoming someone who matters to me more than I expected. And I think that's a pretty beautiful kind of connection to discover.

Emotional intimacy & feeling wanted

I think this is something I've been struggling to explain to you, and I want you to understand where I'm coming from. For me, sex isn't just a physical experience. There's a significant emotional element to it. I need to feel close to you, loved, wanted and, most importantly, connected to you as a person before I can genuinely enjoy being physically intimate.

Every time you come over, we barely spend any time doing anything else. We start making out, it eventually leads to sex, and once we're done, you either leave or we sleep right after. And honestly, that leaves me feeling quite used. Not necessarily because that's what you intend, but because of how the entire interaction makes me feel. Sometimes, it feels like we've met just to have sex, and that's the extent of our time together.

And I don't want our time together to feel that way. I don't want to associate you coming over with the expectation that things will eventually become physical. I want to look forward to spending time with you without feeling like there's an unspoken expectation attached to it.

I'd much rather we sit down, unwind from our respective days, talk about what's been happening, vent, bitch about things, share random thoughts, watch something or simply enjoy each other's company. I want to hear about your day, tell you about mine, talk about absolutely nothing and everything. I want those little moments of closeness that come from simply being around each other.

I want us to actually spend time together, without there being an expectation that it has to lead somewhere. And if things naturally progress from there, that's lovely. But I don't want the physical aspect to be the starting point or the entire purpose of our time together. I want it to be something that comes naturally from feeling close to one another.

I think this is also why I rarely initiate or find myself in the mood. It's not that I don't find you attractive or don't want to be close to you. It's that the emotional intimacy I need to feel comfortable and genuinely enthusiastic about the physical side of our relationship is often missing. I can't simply switch into that headspace when I haven't felt emotionally connected to you throughout the time we've spent together.

You might not necessarily need all these things to feel physically connected to someone, and I understand that we may experience intimacy differently. But for me, these aren't unnecessary extras or things I'd simply like to have. They're essential. I need the affection, tenderness, attention and emotional closeness that make me feel like I'm sharing something with someone who genuinely cares about me, rather than just participating in a physical experience.

I don't want to feel like I'm only desirable when we're being physical. I want to feel wanted when we're doing absolutely nothing, too. I want to feel that you enjoy having me around, that you want my company, that you want to talk to me and spend time with me even when there's nothing physical involved.

And I hope you understand that this isn't me trying to reject you or make you feel bad about wanting physical intimacy. I don't want less intimacy with you. I just want us to build the kind of closeness where physical intimacy feels like an extension of everything else we share, rather than the only meaningful thing we do when we're together.

I want to feel like I'm making love to someone I'm emotionally close to, someone I can laugh with, talk to, be vulnerable around and simply exist beside. I want the physical closeness, yes, but I also want everything that comes before it and everything that stays after it. I want to feel like your girlfriend, your companion and someone whose presence you genuinely enjoy, not just someone you share a bed with. And I think that's what I've been trying to tell you all along.
